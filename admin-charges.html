Ambulance operators & charges

Each ambulance type has its own coloured section below, so nothing gets mixed. Add every operator here: its documents sit inside the operator's own card. Platform fee and GST apply to all of them. Changes show on the website and new invoices immediately.

Platform fee and GST

Common for every ambulance type.

Patient transport charges are GST exempt, so GST is added only on the platform fee. Confirm the current rates with your CA.

Domestic Air Ambulance (Charter Aircraft)

Price = operator's flight charge + the fixed charges below + platform fee + GST.

Charter operators

The first free operator from the top of this list gets the booking. Use Up / Down to change the order.

An operator is free when "Available" is ticked and it has no active booking. A booking keeps its operator until the mission is completed or cancelled. An unpaid booking holds the operator for 15 minutes.

Domestic Commercial Airline Transfer

No operators here. Set the booking amount and base price below, then press Save all changes.

Booking amount and base price

The booking amount is shown to the client on the booking card and is deducted from the final price. The base price and fixed charges below are only our internal estimate, the client does not see them. After airline approval, enter the real prices in Airline Transfers Admin.

Road Ambulance (Van)

Price = base + extra km + add-ons + night, then platform fee + GST. Set here separately; the client sees extra km merged into the base (one line).

Base charge (₹)Extra km (₹ per km)Basic (BLS)
ICU / ALS

Road factor: map distance x this = road distance (1.3 is a good start). Night uses the server's India time. Operator gets % of service total, the rest is your commission (platform fee is extra); clients never see this.

Fixed charges

Added to every road ambulance booking (for example Stretcher, Cleaning, Service charge). The client sees each one as its own line.
